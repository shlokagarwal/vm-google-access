Privacy Policy – vm-google-access

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, vm-google-access can access the Google Drive files and Gmail messages of the account you signed in with, limited to the permissions shown on Google's consent screen.

How the data is used

The data is used only to read, organise, store and send files and email on a private server operated by the developer, for the purposes agreed with the account owner. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except where required by law.

Storage and security

The sign-in token is stored only on that private server, in a location readable only by the server's operator account. Data is transferred to and from Google over encrypted connections.

Google API Services User Data Policy

vm-google-access's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After you revoke it, the app can no longer access your account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via GitHub.
